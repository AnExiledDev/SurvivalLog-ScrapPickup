# Changelog

## 0.1.4

- Clearer wording in the settings and on the Mods screen.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20719.

## 0.1.3

- New setting `Enabled` under Scrap turns the mod on and off. It is on by default. When it is off, items from furniture you scrap or demolish drop like they do without the mod.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20719.

## 0.1.1

- Now needs Exiled's Toolkit 0.19.0 or newer. Settings you change on the Mods screen always apply right away.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20719.

## 0.1.0

- First release.
- While exploring, materials from furniture you scrap or demolish go into your trunk or shopping cart, then your backpack.
- At home, materials from furniture you scrap or demolish go into your backpack.
- Materials drop on the ground only when nothing has room for them.
