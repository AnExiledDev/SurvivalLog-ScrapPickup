# Changelog

## 0.1.2

- New setting `Enabled` under Scrap turns the mod on and off. Off, items from furniture you scrap or demolish drop the way they do without the mod.
- On a new install the mod starts off. Turn on `Enabled` to use it.
- If you already had Scrap Pickup, it stays on after this update.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20719.

## 0.1.1

- Now uses Exiled's Toolkit for its shared parts, so it needs Exiled's Toolkit 0.19.0 or newer. Settings you change on the Mods screen always take effect right away.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20719.

## 0.1.0

- First release.
- While exploring, materials from furniture you scrap or demolish go into your trunk or shopping cart, then your backpack.
- At home, they go into your backpack.
- They only drop on the ground when nothing has room.
