Name: Scrap Pickup
Summary: Items from furniture you scrap or demolish go into your trunk or shopping cart while exploring, then your backpack, instead of falling on the ground. At home they go straight to your backpack. Items drop on the floor only when nothing has room, so you stop chasing glass, metal and wood across the room. Your carry weight still counts.

Description (BBCode):
Demolishing furniture drops its materials on the floor, and scrapping it with a full backpack does the same. Scrap Pickup puts them away for you.

[size=5]Main features[/size]
[list]
[*][b]Trunk first while exploring[/b]: with a shopping cart or car, materials go to its trunk first, then your backpack.[/*]
[*][b]Backpack at home[/b]: the trunk is not with you at home, so materials go to your backpack.[/*]
[*][b]Ground only as a last resort[/b]: items drop on the ground only when the trunk and the backpack have no room.[/*]
[*][b]Both ways of removing furniture[/b]: works for Scrap and for Demolish.[/*]
[/list]
The backpack counts as full when it has no free slot or when the item would put you over your carry weight, the same check the game makes when you pick something up. Only furniture you remove yourself is affected.

Scrap Pickup adds nothing to your save and does not edit game files. You can remove it at any time.

Works in English and Chinese.

[size=5]Known issues[/size]
Items stored inside a piece of furniture (a cabinet's contents, for example) still drop on the ground when you demolish it. Only the materials the furniture breaks down into are put away.

[/list]

[size=5]Installation[/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/survivallog/mods/12]BepInEx[/url] [i](if no other mods were installed before, start the game once so BepInEx finishes setup, then quit)[/i].[/*]

[size=5]Uninstall[/size]
Delete [font=Courier New]ScrapPickup.dll[/font] from the [font=Courier New]BepInEx\plugins[/font] folder.

[size=5]Configuration[/size]
To change settings, edit [font=Courier New]BepInEx\config\com.dev.survivallog.scrappickup.cfg[/font] while the game is closed, or do it in game on the Mod Manager's Mods screen, where changes take effect at once (see [url=https://github.com/AnExiledDev/SurvivalLog-ScrapPickup/blob/main/CONFIG.md]CONFIG.md[/url] for every setting). [font=Courier New]ToTrunk[/font] turns the trunk off; items then go to your backpack. Without Exiled's Toolkit installed, changes made on the Mods screen take effect at the next game start instead.

[size=5]简体中文[/size]

拆除家具时，材料会掉在地上；背包满了时回收家具也一样。Scrap Pickup 会帮你把它们收好。

[size=5]主要功能[/size]
[list]
[*][b]外出时先放后备箱[/b]：有购物车或汽车时，材料先放进它的后备箱，再放进背包。[/*]
[*][b]在家时放背包[/b]：在家时后备箱不在身边，所以材料放进背包。[/*]
[*][b]实在放不下才掉地上[/b]：只有后备箱和背包都没有空间时，物品才会掉在地上。[/*]
[*][b]两种方式都支持[/b]：「回收」和「拆除」家具都有效。[/*]
[/list]
背包没有空格，或者放入物品会超过负重时，就算作已满，和游戏里捡东西时的判断一样。只影响你自己拆掉的家具。

Scrap Pickup 不会往存档里添加任何内容，也不会修改游戏文件，随时可以移除。

支持英文和中文。

[size=5]已知问题[/size]
拆除家具时，家具里存放的物品（比如柜子里的东西）仍会掉在地上。只有家具拆出来的材料会被收好。

[/list]
